# HUMAN.md v0.1.1

Initialization now offers a warmer invitation to gather your thoughts before starting a project. The console message focuses on purpose, curiosity, and sharing context with people and AI collaborators.

- Header: “Give your ideas a place to grow.”
- Next steps: capture what sparked the idea, note why it matters and what you hope to learn, and share your thinking with your team and AI collaborators.
- Closing: “Start small. Revisit as you learn.”

The four templates, CLI commands, offline behavior, zero runtime dependencies, and existing-file protection are unchanged. Color handling, narrow-terminal wrapping, and ASCII fallbacks remain supported.

The GitHub release includes the wheel, source archive, and SHA-256 checksums. Publishing these artifacts to PyPI is a separate step. Once uploaded, update an existing installation with `uv tool upgrade humanmd`.
